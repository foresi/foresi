# foresi/meetings

The Meetings module of [Foresi](https://foresi.app): series of recurring meetings and one-off meetings, with their attendees, the projects and contacts they are about, and their minutes in Markdown. A new meeting of a series starts with the series' agenda template and default attendees; the minutes keep a version, so two people writing at once never overwrite each other.

It requires Work, Party, Identity, Audit, Instance, Shared and Ui. A series is for its members or for the whole firm, the owners see everything, and a guest sees none of it. What it does, who sees what, its events and its ports are in [docs/meetings.md](https://github.com/foresi/foresi/blob/main/docs/meetings.md).
